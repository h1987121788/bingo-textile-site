Order workflow

From qualified brief to repeat order

Every approval creates a record that can be checked again before the next production run.

  1. 01Brief and qualification

    Confirm brand, market, quantity, target cost, timing and reference files.

  2. 02Paid sample plan

    Agree the development scope, sample charge, inputs and expected review date.

  3. 03Fit and wash approval

    Record measurements, construction, hand feel, color and wash comments.

  4. 04Bulk quote and deposit

    Lock the approved specification, packing, trade terms and payment points.

  5. 05Pre-production and QC

    Approve the production reference, follow bulk and record inspection results.

  6. 06Shipment and reorder

    Maintain shipment details, approved references and next-action dates.

Quality control

What must be verified before bulk approval

Quality claims are tied to an order specification and evidence. Certificates, test reports and factory capabilities are only shown when they belong to the selected production source.

No borrowed proof. Sample availability does not prove factory ownership, customer production or bulk capability.
SpecificationComposition, GSM, color and approved material reference
FitMeasurement chart, grading, tolerance and size-set comments
PerformanceShrinkage, colorfastness and pilling checks when required
ConstructionSeams, stitching, hardware, print and embroidery review
PackingLabels, folding, polybag, carton marks and quantity check
TraceabilitySupplier, material, production and shipment records retained per order

Buyer FAQ

Before starting a garment sample

What is the garment MOQ?

MOQ is confirmed after the style, color count, material, wash, decoration and packaging are known. We do not publish an unverified universal MOQ.

Can we add our own labels and artwork?

Yes, subject to supplier capability and an artwork-rights confirmation from the buyer. Label, print, embroidery and packaging details are approved before bulk.

Can development start from a tech pack or sample?

Yes. A tech pack, physical sample, product link or clear reference set can be used to define material, fit and construction.

Does Bingo own the factories shown?

Bingo coordinates selected production and material partners in the Guangzhou supply chain. A partner facility is never presented as owned unless that ownership can be documented.

Are these finished garment samples?

Yes. The current catalog styles correspond to finished garment samples. Current color, size, stock, specification and order terms are reconfirmed before quotation.
