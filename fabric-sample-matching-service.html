What we can match from

A perfect physical swatch is helpful, but many buyers start with incomplete information. We can begin from garment photos, a tech pack, a competitor product, a fabric name, a target GSM or a small cutting sent by courier.

The first goal is to narrow the fabric direction: structure, composition, weight, width, finishing and realistic price level. After that, we can discuss available swatches, lab dips or custom development.

Best information to send first

Garment context

Tell us if the fabric is for T-shirts, hoodies, joggers, fitted tops or kidswear.

Target specs

Share any known GSM, composition, width, stretch, color and finishing requirements.

Commercial target

Estimated quantity, order timing and target price help us choose practical options.

Service workflow

  1. Receive your photo, tech pack, swatch or sample notes.
  2. Identify likely knit structure, composition, GSM and finishing direction.
  3. Compare catalog or supplier options and confirm what can be sampled.
  4. Prepare swatches, lab dips or custom development notes when needed.
  5. Quote bulk fabric by roll, yardage or kg after the direction is approved.
