Development process

Each commercial claim follows an approval

  1. 01Supplier check

    Confirm white-label permission, source data, sample terms and backup options.

  2. 02Physical sample

    Buy and identify the exact sample before publishing specifications.

  3. 03Wear and wash review

    Measure fit, shrinkage, hand feel, shade, construction and hardware.

  4. 04Approved specification

    Freeze measurements, material, colors, trims, labels and packing.

  5. 05Bulk quotation

    Quote the verified MOQ, timing, QC scope, payment and shipment terms.

  6. 06Production record

    Keep approved samples, inspection notes and reorder references.
