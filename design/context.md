# Bingo Garments Local Showroom V2

Audience: independent apparel brands in North America, Europe, Japan, Korea and Singapore.
Goal: see current garments, inspect images and specifications, select styles and send a useful inquiry.
Direction: fashion editorial showroom; white, charcoal, electric blue, restrained lime; product imagery is the focal point.

Acceptance:

- AW26 appears in the first viewport and the first product section. All 20 styles are discoverable in the new catalog.
- Product photographs are primary; full English sheets are optional detail views.
- Three-column desktop grid and one-column phone grid; no horizontal overflow or obscured content.
- Search, category filters, collection tabs, sort, detail dialog, gallery and persistent inquiry selection work.
- Homepage and directory derive collection counts and selections from the same AW26 data.
- Preserve product verification boundaries and quote-only terms. Do not invent specifications, stock, images or prices.
- Keep original CRM form, receipt handling and local-only submit behavior.
- Local preview only. No publishing or outgoing messages.

Flow: Home -> New AW26 -> filter/search -> inspect style/photo/sheet -> add to inquiry -> review selection -> complete brief -> local saved state.
Empty state: no matching products -> clear filters. Invalid form: browser validation retains the brief.
