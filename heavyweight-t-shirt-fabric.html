What buyers usually mean by heavyweight T-shirt fabric

For streetwear brands, heavyweight T-shirt fabric is usually a stable single-knit jersey or double-yarn jersey with enough body for oversized shapes. The buyer is not only asking for a high GSM number; they are asking for a fabric that keeps the garment shape, feels premium, and still has the right drape after washing.

We normally compare GSM, yarn count, cotton quality, shrinkage target, finishing and hand feel against your reference sample before recommending a direction.

Key specs to confirm before quotation

Weight and structure

Share target GSM and whether you prefer compact jersey, double-yarn jersey or a softer drape.

Fiber and stretch

Most projects use cotton or cotton spandex. Elastane changes recovery, width and cost.

Garment fit

Boxy tees, drop shoulder tees and kidswear need different width and hand-feel decisions.
